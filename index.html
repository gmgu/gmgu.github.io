Geonmo Gu

Principal Researcher at LG Electronics

I am a Principal Researcher at LG Electronics, working on Humanoid AI. Previously, I worked on the development of an AI coding assistant built on large language models, used internally across the company.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Kunsoo Park, with a thesis on fast graph isomorphism algorithms. Before joining LG Electronics, I was a postdoctoral research assistant at SNU's Institute of Computer Technology and interned at NAVER.
